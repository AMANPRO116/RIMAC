Interactive engineering model based on the supplied case study. It combines centralized ECU control, BMC authority, motor operation, HV battery switching, pre-charge, DC link, inverter, regenerative braking, LV conversion, protection logic and fault injection.

Source boundary: the supplied report gives ECU/BMC specifications and motor operating data, but it does not provide a complete electrical schematic, switching model or measured vehicle telemetry. Those missing electrical details are explicitly modeled here as engineering assumptions for simulation.
